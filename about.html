Overview

The Angel Protocol Initiative is a research and strategic development division established by Amethyst Corporations. Its primary objective is the creation and maintenance of the Angel Protocol, an advanced artificial intelligence framework intended to provide command support, strategic analysis, autonomous monitoring, and operational decision assistance. The Initiative serves as the corporation's highest-security program, integrating specialists in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, robotics, and systems engineering.

Mission Statement

To develop an adaptive intelligence capable of protecting critical infrastructure, coordinating strategic assets, and assisting human operators through accurate, ethical, and efficient decision support.
